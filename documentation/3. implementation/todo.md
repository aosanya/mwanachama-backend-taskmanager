| W24 | 🚦 **The columns are the lifecycle, not the board's — and `CanTransitionTo` refuses the ordinary kanban moves.** `TaskStatus` is a closed eight (`pending`, `in_progress`, `blocked`, `awaiting-direction`, `completed`, `failed`, `cancelled`, `split`) with a hand-rolled transition table, which is right for an agent-run pipeline and wrong for a board a team arranges: the columns are the same for every project, a team cannot name its own (`Backlog`/`Next`/`Doing`/`Review`/`Done`), and three of the four statuses in that list have no equivalent here at all. Either way it is a decision to record, not a default to inherit. | 📋 | Filed 2026-09-28, same pass as W23.
